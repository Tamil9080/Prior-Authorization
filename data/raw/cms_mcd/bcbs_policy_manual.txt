Blue Cross Blue Shield Association Medical Policy: Knee Reconstruction.

BCBS covers Total Knee Arthroplasty (CPT 27447) when the following criteria are documented:
1. Moderate-to-severe joint narrowing or osteoarthritis (KL Grade 3 or 4).
2. Documented functional impairment score using a validated clinical assessment scale. Validated scales include the Oxford Knee Score (OKS), the Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index (WOMAC), or the Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score (KOOS).
3. Inadequate response to at least 12 weeks of non-surgical conservative treatment (injections, physical therapy, or weight loss program).
4. Exclusion: Joint replacement without a documented functional impairment score (WOMAC, OKS, or KOOS) will be pended.
